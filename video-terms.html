These terms apply to one-off video editing orders ("Reel edit" and "Rush Reel") bought from Socials by Emiliya, run by Emiliya Sivankova ("I", "me"). "You" means the person or business placing the order. By ticking the box at checkout you agree to these terms. Monthly social media packages have their own client agreement.

1. What you get

  • One edited video of up to 60 seconds in vertical 9:16 format, delivered as an MP4, unless you buy add-ons or we agree something else in writing.
  • Captions and on-screen text, logo and sponsor animation, and royalty-free music or no music, as chosen on your brief.
  • Two rounds of revisions. A round is one list of changes to the same edit, sent in one message.

2. What I need from you

  • Full payment upfront, a completed brief and all your files.
  • Feedback on each draft within 14 days. If I don't hear back within 14 days of sending a draft, the order is treated as complete and I'll send the final file.

3. Turnaround

  • Reel edit: first draft within 3 working days. Rush Reel: first draft within 24 hours. Revisions to a Rush Reel are returned within 24 hours.
  • Turnaround starts once I have your payment, brief and all files. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.
  • If you send new files or change the brief after I've started, the turnaround restarts.
  • If I can't take on a Rush Reel, I'll tell you within 12 hours of your order and either refund you in full or move you to a standard Reel edit and refund the difference, whichever you prefer.

4. Extra work

Anything outside the order is charged separately and agreed with you before I start: extra formats (£60 each), a teaser cut (£60), extra revision rounds (£50 each), longer edits (from £450), or a new idea, new footage or a different length after the first draft.

5. Your content and permissions

  • You confirm that you own, or have permission to use, everything you send me, including footage, photos, logos, music, and any clips from YouTube, Instagram or other sites, and that the people shown are happy to appear.
  • I don't check the rights to material you supply. If a claim is made because of material you sent, you're responsible for it, not me.
  • Music I add comes from royalty-free libraries licensed for social media use. Platforms can still occasionally flag tracks; if that happens I'll swap the track for free.

6. AI tools

I may use AI tools, for example to animate a still image or add motion. Their cost is included in the price, and I'll tell you where I've used them. If you'd rather I didn't use AI, say so on your brief.

7. Ownership and use

  • Once you've paid in full, you can use the final video however you like, on any platform, for as long as you like.
  • I keep the editing project files and any templates I create. Raw project files aren't included unless agreed in writing.
  • I may show the finished video in my portfolio and on my social media, unless you opt out on your brief or by email.

8. Cancellations and refunds

  • Cancel before I start editing: full refund.
  • Cancel after I've started but before the first draft: 50% refund.
  • After the first draft is sent: no refund, but you still get your revision rounds and the final video.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 working days.

9. Files and storage

I'll keep your files and the final video for 30 days after delivery, then delete them. Please save your final video somewhere safe.
