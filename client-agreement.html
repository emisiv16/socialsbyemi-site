Client Agreement

Last updated 9 November 2026

1. Who this agreement is between

This agreement is between Emiliya Sivankova, trading as Socials by Emiliya ("I", "me"), hello@emiliyasivankova.co.uk, and the business that buys a monthly package ("you").

Your package, monthly fee and platforms are the ones in the proposal I email you and the payment link you pay through. Your start date is the date of your first payment. If this agreement and your proposal disagree, this agreement wins.

You accept this agreement by ticking the box at checkout. The version that applies to you is the one on this page on the day you first pay.

2. What I'll do

2.1 I'll provide the social media services in your proposal with reasonable skill and care.

2.2 Anything not listed in your proposal is extra. I'll quote it in writing first, and only start once you've agreed.

2.3 If I miss any of the month's posts through my own fault, I'll add them to the next month. Posts missed because footage, information, access or approvals arrived late don't carry over.

3. How the first three months work

Each of the first three months has its own goal:

  1. Month one: strategy. Discovery call, research into your audience and competitors, an audit of what you've posted so far, and your content strategy. Posting may start towards the end of month one.
  2. Month two: testing. Full posting starts. We test content, formats and timings to see what works.
  3. Month three: refining. The plan is refined using what we learned in month two.

The number of posts in your package applies from month two. In month one, the fee covers the strategy and set-up work.

4. How long the agreement lasts

4.1 The minimum term is 3 months from the start date.

4.2 After that, it continues month to month. Either of us can end it by giving 30 days' notice in writing; email is fine. The agreement then ends on the last day of the payment month in which the 30 days run out. You can give notice during the minimum term, so that the agreement ends when the minimum term does.

4.3 If you end the agreement during the minimum term, the fees for the rest of the minimum term are still due.

4.4 Either of us can end the agreement by email if the other seriously breaks it and doesn't put it right within 14 days of being asked to. If the breach can't be put right, the agreement can end straight away. Serious breaches include, for example:

  • an invoice still unpaid 14 days after its due date
  • repeatedly not providing access, footage, information or approvals, so the work can't be delivered
  • abusive, threatening or harassing behaviour
  • asking for content that is illegal, misleading or against platform rules
  • not delivering the agreed work for 2 weeks or more without a good reason
  • misusing the other's confidential information or account access

4.5 If I end the agreement because of your serious breach, the fees for the rest of the minimum term are still due.

5. Fees and payment

5.1 Fees are paid monthly in advance. You make your first payment through the payment link I send, and Stripe then takes each month's fee automatically on the same date each month. Work begins once the first month is paid. If your card details change, email me and I'll send you a secure link to update them.

5.2 All fees are in pounds sterling (GBP). Any bank or currency conversion charges on your side are yours to cover.

5.3 If a payment is more than 7 days late, I'll pause the work, including scheduled posts, until it's paid. The paused time still counts towards the minimum term.

5.4 Your monthly fee is fixed for 12 months from the start date, even if my prices for new clients go up. After that, I may change it by giving you 30 days' notice in writing.

5.5 I'm not currently registered for VAT. If I register, VAT will be added to future invoices at the rate that applies.

5.6 Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta (or the relevant platform) and isn't included in my fees. Ad accounts stay in your name. I'll never spend more than the budget you approve in writing.

5.7 Fees already paid aren't refundable, unless I haven't delivered the work for that month.

6. When the agreement ends

Within 7 days of the agreement ending, I'll:

  • send you your content calendar and the final files I made for you
  • remove my access to your accounts, or ask you to remove it
  • cancel your monthly payments and send a final invoice for anything still owed

Posts I've already scheduled before the end date will still go out unless you ask me to cancel them.

If you'd like to work together again after the agreement has ended, we'll sign a new agreement at my prices at that time, with a new 3-month minimum term.

7. What I need from you

7.1 Photos and footage, following the monthly shot list, by the dates we agree.

7.2 Access to your accounts through the platforms' own team or partner access (for example Meta Business Suite). Please don't send passwords by email or message.

7.3 Information I need to do the job well: your offers, prices, timetable, events and any changes to them, with reasonable notice.

7.4 One main contact who can answer questions and approve content.

If material, access or feedback arrives late, I'll do what I can with what I have. Delays on your side don't reduce the monthly fee.

8. Approvals and changes

8.1 I'll send content for your approval at least 5 working days before it's due to go live.

8.2 Each post includes up to 2 rounds of changes: you send feedback within 2 working days, I send the updated version within 1 working day, and you send any final changes within 1 working day. Once those rounds are used, any further changes go into the next month's content.

8.3 If I haven't heard back within 2 working days, I'll check in once. If there's still no reply, I'll post everyday content as planned so your accounts stay consistent. Anything that mentions prices, offers, dates or promotions will only go live once you've approved it.

8.4 Once a month's content calendar is approved and scheduled, any new or last-minute requests go into the following week's content.

9. Communication and working hours

9.1 I work Monday to Friday and reply to emails and messages within 1 working day. Weekend work isn't part of any package.

9.2 Comments and DMs are handled Monday to Friday, as set out in your package.

9.3 Calls are booked in advance through my booking link, for the length set out in your package.

9.4 If something urgent comes up on your accounts, such as a complaint that's gaining attention, I'll flag it to your main contact the same working day. Any public response needs your OK.

9.5 Calls cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed, aren't rescheduled that month.

9.6 Calls end when the booked time ends. If we agree to run over, or you'd like calls beyond your package, the extra time is billed at £80 an hour, in 15-minute blocks.

10. Content and ownership

10.1 Once you've paid for the month, you own the final posts, captions and edited videos I make for you that month.

10.2 I keep ownership of my own templates, methods, frameworks and know-how, and can keep using them for other clients.

10.3 You confirm that you have the right to use everything you send me (photos, footage, music, logos) and that anyone who appears in it, such as members or staff, has agreed to be shown on social media. You're responsible for any claims that arise from material you supply.

10.4 I'll only mention you or show your work in my portfolio or marketing with your written permission.

10.5 I'll only use music and sounds that are licensed for business use on each platform.

11. Confidentiality and data

11.1 We'll both keep each other's confidential information private, during the agreement and after it ends.

11.2 Account access is used only for this work, kept secure and removed when the agreement ends.

11.3 We'll both follow the data protection laws that apply to us (such as UK GDPR or EU GDPR) when handling personal information, such as messages from your followers.

12. My team

12.1 I may use trusted freelancers to help deliver the work. They work under the Socials by Emiliya name, sign confidentiality agreements and work with you only through me. I stay responsible for their work and remain your only point of contact.

12.2 You agree not to hire or work directly with anyone who has worked on your account through me, during this agreement and for 12 months after it ends, unless I agree in writing.

13. Results

I'll use my skills and experience to grow your social media, but I can't guarantee specific numbers of followers, views, enquiries or sales. Results depend on many things outside my control, including platform algorithm changes, your offer and how quickly enquiries are followed up.

14. Liability

14.1 I'm not responsible for losses caused by things outside my control, including platform outages, policy changes, or account restrictions that aren't caused by my work.

14.2 I'm not liable for indirect losses, such as lost profits or lost business.

14.3 My total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid me in the 3 months before the claim.

14.4 Nothing in this agreement limits liability that can't be limited by law.

15. General

15.1 This agreement and your proposal are the whole agreement between us. Any changes must be agreed in writing; email is fine. Notices under this agreement, including notice to end it, can be sent by email: to me at hello@emiliyasivankova.co.uk, and to you at the email you paid with.

15.2 I'm an independent contractor, not your employee.

15.3 Neither of us can pass this agreement to someone else without the other's written agreement.

15.4 This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales will deal with any dispute.

15.5 You accept this agreement by ticking the box at checkout before your first payment. That counts the same as a signature. If you'd prefer a signed copy as well, just ask.

15.6 "Working days" means Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays. Deadlines in this agreement are in UK time.
