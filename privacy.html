Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Socials by Emiliya is run by Emiliya Sivankova ("I", "me"). I'm responsible for the personal information you share with me. This policy explains what I collect, why, and what your rights are under UK data protection law.

What I collect

  • Enquiries and briefs: your name, email, business name, Instagram handle and anything you write in my website forms.
  • Bookings: your name, email and answers to booking questions when you book a call through Cal.com.
  • Payments: your name, email and billing details, handled by Stripe. I never see or store your full card details.
  • Project files: footage, photos, logos and other material you send me to work on.
  • Messages: emails and Instagram messages you send me, including automatic replies such as the free posting plan.
  • Call recordings: with your permission, recordings and notes of Strategy Plan and client calls, used only to write your plan or notes.
  • Client work: if you're a client, access details and information about your business and social media accounts.

Why I use it

  • To reply to you, deliver the service you've bought and send you the things you've asked for (performing a contract with you).
  • To take payments and keep financial records (legal obligation).
  • To run and improve my business, for example showing finished work in my portfolio unless you opt out (legitimate interests).

I don't sell your information, and I won't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Who I share it with

Only the services I use to run the business: Netlify (website and forms), Google Workspace (email and file storage), Stripe (payments), Cal.com and Zoom (bookings and calls), Notuly (call notes), WeTransfer, Google Drive or Dropbox (file transfers), and Meta and ManyChat (Instagram messages). Some of these store data outside the UK, with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses in place.

How long I keep it

  • Video editing files: deleted 30 days after delivery.
  • Call recordings: deleted within 30 days of sending your plan or notes.
  • Enquiries and briefs that don't lead to work: up to 2 years.
  • Client records, invoices and payment records: 6 years, as required for tax.

Cookies

My website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. Stripe and Cal.com may set their own essential cookies when you pay or book.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you, object to how I use it, or ask me to send it to you. Email hello@emiliyasivankova.co.uk and I'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
