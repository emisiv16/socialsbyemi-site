FAQ

Do you work with gyms outside the UK?

Yes. I work remotely with sports and fitness venues worldwide. Prices are in pounds sterling.

What if my members don't want to be filmed?

That's fine. I plan content that doesn't need faces, like coaches, equipment, hands and feet, and wide shots, and only feature members who've said yes.

How soon will I see results?

Month one is strategy: I audit your accounts and build your plan. Posting starts by month two, when we test what works, and by month three everything is refined and running.
