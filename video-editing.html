FAQ

Good to know

What can I send you?

Video clips, photos, logos, sponsor logos, brand colours and fonts, and links to YouTube or Instagram videos you already have permission to use. Phone footage is absolutely fine.

Can you use clips from YouTube?

Yes, as long as you own them or have permission to use them, for example your own competition footage or clips the organiser has cleared. You confirm this on the brief, because I can't check the rights for you.

What counts as a revision?

One round is one list of changes to the same edit: swap a clip, change text, tweak timing, change the music. A new idea, new footage or a different length after the first draft is a new request.

Do you use AI?

Sometimes, for things like animating a still photo or a mascot. It's included in the price and I'll always tell you where I've used it.

When do the 3 working days start?

Once I have your payment, your brief and all your files. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, if you cancel before I start editing. Everything is in the video editing terms.
